# Paid early unlock with a charity share

Research dated 18 November 2026. Scope is Danish adult consumers using their own phone on the **Myself** profile. The proposed purchase shortens one wait-time key to about ten minutes. It never applies to child phones or overrides a buddy or trusted-person key.

**Decision: go with changes, subject to launch gates.** Treat the unlock as a one-off digital sale and the advertised allocation as a Danish fundraising campaign. Use ordinary store billing for store builds, start with a fixed price, and commit a specific amount from the VAT-inclusive price to the selected charity. Keep the feature off until counsel confirms the arrangement, the necessary permit covers the checkout and settlement, and the relevant payment provider or store accepts the actual flow. If those conditions cannot be met, keep paid unlock and fundraising separate, with no promised purchase-linked share. A separate donation must confer no unlock or other benefit.

Confidence labels indicate how directly the evidence answers the question. **High** means an express rule or clear official guidance, **Medium** means a reasoned application to this design, and **UNVERIFIED** flags evidence or an approval this research could not establish.

## 2. Danish and EU law

### Fundraising

**High.** Indsamlingsloven § 1(3) expressly covers sales when the seller announces in immediate connection with the sale that part of the purchase price goes to a predefined purpose. A paid digital benefit is compatible with that definition. Selecting a named beneficiary before payment makes the purpose identifiable. Offering it to ordinary app customers is a public solicitation even behind login. The relevant Danish equivalent to a commercial co-venture is *indsamling gennem salg*. Red Barnet publicly describes collaborations with businesses through sales under its fundraising arrangements. Another charity’s permit does not automatically cover Mahope. [Indsamlingsloven §§ 1–4](https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2026/867/pdf), [Civilstyrelsen sales FAQ](https://www.civilstyrelsen.dk/sagsomraader/indsamlingsnaevnet/ofte-stillede-spoergsmaal), [Red Barnet accounts and permits](https://redbarnet.dk/om-red-barnet/budget-aarsregnskab-og-indsamlingstilladelser/)

**High on permit requirement, Medium on the applicant and structure.** The working route is a specific § 3 permission before soliciting, for up to one year at a time. The § 4 route is for qualifying organisations approved to receive deductible gifts and in existence at least three years. The application must specify organiser, purpose, timing, geography, payment methods and other required details. The order requires a dedicated bank account for collected funds under the ordinary § 3 route, restricts diversion to other purposes, and allows reasonable collection expenses. Counsel should resolve whether one § 3 permission can cover ten named purposes and whether a platform can first settle blended receipts into a merchant account before the charity amount goes to a dedicated account. These points are **UNVERIFIED**. [Civilstyrelsen application guidance](https://www.civilstyrelsen.dk/sagsomraader/indsamlingsnaevnet/ansoeg-digitalt-om-tilladelse-til-indsamling-efter-%C2%A7-3-eller-%C2%A7-4), [Fundraising order §§ 2, 6–8](https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2026/912/pdf)

### Marketing and accounts

**High.** Under Markedsføringsloven §§ 5–8 and 13, disclose material terms without misleading presentation and substantiate factual claims. The Consumer Ombudsman’s guidance on ethical claims calls for a clear description of how support works and the most precise amount possible, preferably in kroner and øre. Good fundraising practice §§ 23.1–23.2 calls for the share of price, turnover or profit and disclosure of the minimum in case of loss. Show the exact amount to the named recipient before payment and state the calculation base. If using a net formula, identify the deductions for VAT, fees and any other costs and show the actual DKK amount before purchase. For a gross promise, Mahope absorbs VAT and fees from the remainder and guarantees the stated amount even at a loss. Keep records of what was advertised, refunded and paid. [Markedsføringsloven](https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2024/1420), [Consumer Ombudsman guidance, § 9.3](https://forbrugerombudsmanden.dk/publikationer/retningslinjer-og-vejledninger/vejledninger-longreads/vejledning-om-brug-af-miljoemaessige-og-etiske-paastande-mv), [Good fundraising practice](https://www.civilstyrelsen.dk/html-publikation/retningslinjer-for-god-indsamlingsskik)

**High.** File the § 3 campaign accounts within six months after it ends and publish signed accounts on the website. Account for proceeds and their use, including any collection expenses. Civilstyrelsen gives DKK 50,000 as the 2026 audit threshold, with an audit required above that amount. For applications from 27 October 2026, the annually adjusted threshold in force at campaign close applies. Confirm the threshold for the actual closing year and follow any outstanding-funds reporting obligations. The voluntary quarterly report below supplements these filings. [Civilstyrelsen accounting guidance](https://www.civilstyrelsen.dk/sagsomraader/indsamlingsnaevnet/regnskabsaflaeggelse)

### VAT and tax deduction

**High on direct-sale VAT, Medium on contractual settlement.** The compulsory checkout price is consideration for a taxable supply. For a Danish domestic direct sale, at 25% VAT, DKK 10 includes DKK 2 VAT. A DKK 5 charity commitment leaves DKK 3 after VAT and before payment fees. At DKK 20 and 40 the corresponding amounts are DKK 6 and 12 before fees. The transfer does not reduce the VAT base. Mahope therefore does not retain 50% in net revenue. Google says it determines, charges and remits EU customer VAT for Play Billing, while the developer handles it for alternative billing. Reconcile at transaction level and have the accountant confirm Apple’s contract, invoicing and tax chain and any other VAT exposure, including EU cross-border rules if the pilot expands. [Momsloven §§ 27 and 33](https://www.retsinformation.dk/api/pdf/241297), [Google tax guidance](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/138000?hl=en), [Apple agreements](https://developer.apple.com/support/terms/apple-developer-program-license-agreement/)

**High on general deduction rules, Medium on Mahope’s treatment.** If these are Mahope’s own qualifying gifts, § 8 A allows a deduction subject to an aggregate limit (DKK 20,000 for 2026), current recipient approval and the recipient’s reporting of the gift under the actual donor’s identifier. § 12(2)–(3) is a distinct route for binding ongoing obligations to specifically approved recipients, with its own income-based limits and exceptions, rather than a way to deduct ordinary monthly remittances under a sales promise. Confirm the identity and tax status of the taxpayer behind the CVR, whether sums held for a campaign are Mahope’s own gifts at all, and whether a real sponsorship with consideration would instead be assessed as a business expense. Model zero tax relief until confirmed. The consumer buys an unlock and should receive a purchase receipt with no claim of a personal donation deduction. Foreign nonprofit registration alone supplies no Danish approval. [Ligningsloven §§ 8 A and 12](https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2025/1500/pdf), [Skattestyrelsen legal guidance](https://info.skat.dk/data.aspx?oid=2061724), [Deduction and reporting guidance](https://skat.dk/borger/fradrag/fradrag-for-gaver-og-bidrag-til-velgoerende-foreninger)

### Withdrawal and digital delivery

**High on law, Medium on classification.** A consumer normally has 14 days from contracting for a distance-sold service, subject to the rules tying the period to proper disclosure. The unlock is most plausibly a non-financial service changing an existing setting. Under Forbrugeraftaleloven § 18(2)(2), obtain prior express consent to start early and acknowledgement that the right ends upon full performance. Treat the actual successful unlock as the earliest point of full performance. During the ten-minute wait provide a cancel/refund path. § 25 permits proportionate charges in qualifying cases, but a full refund before delivery is the proposed policy. If counsel classifies it as non-physical digital content, § 18(2)(13) instead requires prior express consent, acknowledgement of loss when performance begins and durable confirmation of both. Give precontract disclosures, use an unambiguous payment button, record a separate unticked affirmative choice, and provide durable confirmation with the terms and consent. Preserve statutory remedy and platform refund rights. Confirm current Danish implementation of the EU online withdrawal-function requirement and where the control belongs for store checkouts. [Forbrugeraftaleloven §§ 8, 12–13, 18–19, 25](https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2025/1184/pdf), [Directive (EU) 2023/2673, Art. 11a](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2023/2673/oj)

### Escalation, gambling and unfair design

**High on gambling risk being low for the described mechanism.** A fixed, certain result has no chance-based prize or wager on an outcome. Avoid random rewards, sweepstakes or prize pools. [Spillemyndigheden licensing criteria](https://spillemyndigheden.dk/virksomheder-og-foreninger/ulovligt-spil/sanktioner-og-anmeldelse-af-ulovligt-spil)

**High on governing test, Medium on application.** UCPD Arts. 5–9 and Danish §§ 4–8 cover misleading presentation and omissions, aggressive practices and exploitation of vulnerable consumers. A known, deterministic price ladder has no automatic exemption or identified per se ban under those rules. Pressure at the point of lockout, hidden price steps or reset times, a deliberately buried free exit, charity guilt and repeat purchase prompts would materially raise risk. DSA Art. 25 targets interfaces of online platforms, defined in Art. 3 by user-information storage and dissemination to the public, and excludes practices covered by UCPD or GDPR. A private phone-control checkout likely falls outside that online-platform definition, subject to what the whole service offers. [Commission UCPD guidance, § 4.2.7](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/PDF/?uri=CELEX%3A52021XC1229%2805%29), [DSA Arts. 3 and 25](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2022/2065/oj)

**High on proposal status, Medium on analogies.** The Commission proposed the Digital Fairness Act on 11 November 2026. Procedure 2026/0356(COD) is pending. The proposal addresses manipulative interfaces, harmful profiled pricing for minors or vulnerable consumers, and spending-maximising mechanics including virtual currencies and variable rewards. Do not assume that a focus timer is a retail virtual queue, that an unlock is a game mechanic, or that a repeat price based on account history is exempt from disclosure for automated personalised pricing. Have counsel review the actual schedule and tracking. Start with a fixed price, a hard cap of one purchase per rolling seven days, advance opt-in while unlocked, and a prominent free wait option. If escalation is revisited, publish all price steps, cap and reset time in advance and repeat them before checkout. Keep emergency access and essential device recovery free and outside the paywall. [Commission proposal](https://commission.europa.eu/document/download/0575d59f-554f-4a9e-b54b-e6b717175809_en?filename=Proposal+for+a+Digital+Fairness+Act_0.pdf), [Parliament procedure](https://oeil.europarl.europa.eu/en/procedure-file?reference=2026/0356(COD))

## 5. Recommended checkout and reporting

**Payment and money flow.** Pilot in Denmark. For a store build, the platform processes the one-off digital purchase and settles with Mahope under the applicable agreement. For an independently distributed Android build or standalone web checkout, Mahope as seller can use Stripe if approved. In either case Mahope manages the sales proceeds and remits the promised share to the selected legal entity, with the charity portion accounted for in the dedicated campaign account according to the approved settlement flow. Do not use split payouts or let a charity take payment for the unlock without a separately cleared legal and provider structure. A failed, duplicate or undeliverable unlock triggers a refund, with no repeat charge on retries. If store review rejects the bundled arrangement, leave it disabled on that store and reassess a separate direct-to-charity model. Only offer an unlock when it actually saves time.

**Proposed English purchase copy** for a DKK 10 fixed-price purchase. Use the real times, selected beneficiary and exact displayed checkout price. Publish a Danish equivalent before a Danish launch. The example assumes service classification, the proposed cap and that campaign terms allow refund adjustment.

> **End this wait early**
>
> Unlock at 15:10, 10 minutes after payment. Or wait until 16:00 for free.
>
> One-time price DKK 10, including VAT. Choose who receives DKK 5.
>
> Mahope (CVR 39413892) will pay DKK 5 to Børns Vilkår for this purchase, half of the price including VAT. VAT and payment fees come out of Mahope’s share. Refunded purchases are excluded.
>
> Available once every seven days for your own wait-time key on Myself. Buddy and trusted-person keys still require their approval.
>
> ☐ I request that the early-unlock service starts now, before the 14-day withdrawal period ends. I understand that I lose my right to withdraw once the service has been fully performed and the early unlock has been delivered.
>
> **Pay DKK 10** · **Keep waiting for free**
>
> Withdrawal and refunds · Giving and campaign accounts · Seller details

Before a recipient is selected, show an unselected list. After selection, render the named promise and amount, with no preselected consent. Post-purchase text during delivery: “Your early unlock is scheduled for 15:10. Cancel early unlock and request a refund.” If the store cannot support the final legal consent, payment acknowledgement and withdrawal flow, do not rely on a waiver there. Where a digital-content classification is confirmed, substitute a separate consent such as “I expressly agree to delivery starting before the 14-day withdrawal period ends. I understand that I lose my right to withdraw when delivery begins,” and confirm that consent on a durable medium.

**Public reporting.** At `plainphone.org/giving`, give the organiser, permit reference and dates, complaints link to Indsamlingsnævnet, approved purposes, recipient legal names, formula, payout cadence and refund policy. Publish quarterly totals by recipient, separating gross paid, refunded amounts, promised charity share, actual transfers with dates and outstanding balances. Reconcile VAT, store or processor fees and merchant proceeds separately. Post signed statutory campaign accounts and applicable auditor or surplus statements. Keep payout acknowledgements and reconciliation records privately, and publish only aggregate figures or redacted organisational receipts. Document any rounding for non-even øre prices before publishing a percentage promise. Pay on a regular short cadence subject to the permit and record post-payout chargebacks without silently reducing future transactions’ advertised share.

**Questions for Danish counsel and the accountant before launch**

1. Who is the legal collector and seller on each channel? Does § 3 cover all ten designated purposes in one permission, and how must digital settlements, funds segregation, international remittances, refunds and any beneficiary change be handled?
2. Can the gross pledge and public marketing run under the proposed permit and trade descriptions? Confirm the reporting dates, audit threshold, loss guarantee and written beneficiary agreements.
3. Confirm the VAT base, platform contract and settlement chain, refund accounting, donor of record and any available income-tax treatment under § 8 A, § 12 or genuine sponsorship. Check whether the chosen payment flow creates regulated intermediary activity.
4. Confirm distance-sale classification, the moment of full performance, localized disclosures and the withdrawal/refund interface. Review repeat-pricing disclosure, vulnerable-adult protection, safety access and EU Digital Fairness Act developments before expansion.
5. Confirm store and payment-provider acceptance independently. The interactions between Apple IAP and its charity rules, and between a store’s settlement account and the Danish dedicated campaign account, are material unresolved dependencies.
