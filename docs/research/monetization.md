# Plainphone monetization and the case for staying free

18 November 2026 · Decision brief

Keep the Android app, source, and one fully protected hosted phone per household free. Keep the chosen DKK 29/month household plan for additional hosted phones as the default after the 10–20-family trial. Test whether it pays for work Mahope actually needs to do. A completely free hosted service is viable at small scale if Mahope explicitly budgets for it. Continuing that model indefinitely depends on recurring unrestricted funding or a deliberate owner subsidy.

**Evidence key.** **High** means a primary source or the current repository directly supports the statement. **Medium** means an inference or a rough model built from sourced inputs. **Low** means a planning assumption. **UNVERIFIED** marks claims for which direct evidence is missing, including local willingness to pay, future award decisions and production capacity. Public prices are snapshots in the displayed currency and market. They exclude unconfirmed local taxes, shipping and checkout promotions.

## 1. Can everything stay free?

Free hosting removes a decision at setup, treats single- and multi-child households alike, and avoids subscription engineering and billing support. It also fits the public promise cleanly. The practical question is who funds uptime, security patches, recovery and family support over several years. Free software already has a durable route independent of the hosted business if deployment remains documented and interoperable. The trial should establish whether households can actually use that route or whether free access depends on managed hosting. ### Monthly infrastructure and work

**High on architecture.** The starting point is one host, with a Hono API, Postgres, web dashboard, SMTP mail and a separate DNS stack. AdGuard Home serves DNS-over-TLS for registered ClientIDs. ppdns provisions client profiles and refreshes lists. Query logging is disabled in generated config and statistics are enabled with a 24-hour interval. Audit statistics and retention before public scale, and use aggregate operations measurements without domain histories, analytics or individual usage tracking. Android requests periodic sync every 15 minutes, subject to scheduling and connectivity. Count profile configuration churn, TLS connection establishment, backups and restore time in addition to raw DNS QPS. **Low, UNVERIFIED load assumptions.** Treat all N phones as active on hosted DNS. Use 500–3,000 client queries/phone/day (base 1,000), 30 days, and 3–5× mean as a peak envelope. Mean QPS = N × queries/day ÷ 86,400. A rough 0.5–1.5 KB total per query with reused connections yields 15–45 MB/phone/month at base volume, before handshake spikes and upstream traffic. Upstream work depends on cache hit rate and filtering. Allow one periodic sync opportunity every 15 minutes (96/phone/day), before multiple API calls per cycle, retries, manual updates and batching. Mail assumes 0.2–2 successful sends/active phone/month, including signup, invitations and recovery averaged over the active base. Launch and incident spikes can exceed that range.

### Donation break-even

**Low, scenario only.** Use active phones as a proxy for donor reach, and count unique donors. Assume 0.5% of active phones yield recurring equivalent donors and DKK 25 net/donor/month, or DKK 0.125/phone/month. A sensitivity of 0.1–1% gives DKK 0.025–0.25/phone/month. Deduct actual payment and fiscal-host costs before using this formula. Separately value maintainer time at DKK 500/hour, with illustrative monthly capacity of 8, 25 and 100 hours respectively. This includes ordinary support, updates and operations. It excludes major feature development and separately staffed on-call cover.

| Phones | Donations/month at 0.5% | Infra-only donor rate needed | Work allowance | Total DKK/month | Donor rate needed including work |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1,000 | 125 | 0.40–2.20% | 4,000 | 4,100–4,550 | 16.4–18.2% |
| 10,000 | 1,250 | 0.16–0.66% | 12,500 | 12,900–14,150 | 5.16–5.66% |
| 100,000 | 12,500 | 0.094–0.434% | 50,000 | 52,350–60,850 | 2.09–2.43% |

Donations alone can already fall short at 1k, may cover hosting at 10k, and could cover the modeled hosting range at 100k if donor conversion holds. They miss labour at every modeled scale, even at 1% conversion. At 0.1%, income is DKK 25, 250 and 2,500 respectively, covering only the very low end of the 100k infrastructure estimate. There is no universal failure scale. The threshold is recurring net gifts below monthly bills plus the owner's agreed time budget. If there are 1.5 phones per household and each household contributes at most once, required donor percentages per household are 1.5× the percentages above. **UNVERIFIED** actual conversion, gifts and support demand. Use a rolling three-month ledger and cash reserves rather than treating a grant or launch spike as recurring income.

## 2. Commercial models

### Store, privacy and unit economics

- **Google Play, high on policy.** In-app sales of hosted digital entitlements generally require Play Billing unless a specific program or exception applies. Offer Stripe on the standalone web checkout. Do not route users there from the Play app by default. An in-app Stripe option for eligible EEA users requires program enrollment, prescribed disclosures, transaction reporting and applicable Play fees. Verify rules for each storefront at launch. Model 15% for auto-renewing Play subscriptions. Physical goods and services are outside the digital purchase requirement. - **iOS, high on published wording.** Review §§3.1.1–3.1.4. Multiplatform digital access generally requires an equivalent IAP option under §3.1.3(b). The enterprise-only, free companion, physical goods and real-time one-to-one exceptions each have conditions. Consumer family access in a mixed B2B/B2C app warrants separate review. EU alternatives have their own terms and fees. **UNVERIFIED** how review will classify Plainphone's parent app and a managed iOS child experience. Check Apple capabilities separately before promising Android parity. [Apple App Review Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/) · [Apple EU terms](https://developer.apple.com/support/apps-in-the-eu)
- **Illustrative contribution, medium arithmetic.** At DKK 29 including 25% Danish VAT, DKK 23.20 remains before payment fees. Illustrative Play contribution is DKK 19.72 after 15% of the ex-VAT price. Standard EEA Stripe card processing at 1.5% of gross + DKK 1.80 leaves DKK 20.97, before Stripe Billing or Tax, refunds, disputes and support. Use DKK 20 per paid household-month as a planning proxy. Obtain actual settlements and confirm VAT/OSS, platform tax responsibility, receipts and grant/donation treatment with the accountant. [Danish standard VAT](https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/document/download/458ad55a-611b-4542-82e5-8b2085934136_en?filename=moss_2018_dk_en.pdf) · [Stripe Denmark card fees](https://stripe.com/en-dk/pricing/local-payment-methods)
- **Hard boundaries.** Never run ads, sell data, add tracking/analytics, fingerprinting or marketing cookies, or paywall safety features. Keep emergency calling, essential contacts, core filtering and safe recovery available without payment. Put adult billing on an adult-facing surface. Minimise billing records, keep processors away from child data and disable marketing pixels and email open/click tracking. Paid capacity requires a safe downgrade: retain existing protections, notice and a grace period, then stop adding new hosted slots while offering a safe migration or funded exception. Free one-phone access needs a documented transfer/replacement path. A sponsor can be acknowledged in a public funding ledger without advertising to children. ### Adult digital minimalism

**High on the study, low on the market inference.** A 2025 randomised intervention found improvements in attention, mental health and subjective wellbeing after blocking mobile internet for two weeks. Sustained adherence was difficult. Calls and texts remained available, making the experiment relevant to the Myself concept, without validating Plainphone's particular controls, wait lengths or outcomes. Products like Opal, one sec and Light Phone show an active category with both software and hardware approaches. Regional growth, long-term retention and adult willingness to pay are **UNVERIFIED**. ## 4. Recommendation and 12-month sequence

1. **Hosted freemium.** Start with a fully protected free slot and model DKK 29/month for added hosting. Ship billing only after the trial and an explicit decision gate. The strongest reason to charge is ongoing support and reliable operation, especially as a one-person company takes on security and availability obligations.
2. **Optional paid setup.** A small capped, adult-facing service tests whether setup friction is itself worth paying to remove, with minimal build and no change to free safety. Proceed only if sessions fit the available owner time and price.
3. **Funded public-interest work and voluntary contributions.** Prefer a defined open-source deliverable or independent trial evaluation and optional funding links on adult web/repo surfaces. Book grants as zero until committed. Consider a partner-funded cohort after repeatable onboarding and evidence of institutional demand.

**Should it just stay free?** Yes for software and one hosted phone. Keep all hosting free during the trial. A permanently free managed service is a defensible public-interest choice if Mahope can ring-fence the next 12 months of realistic infrastructure and owner time, and keep replacements and recovery dependable. At 1,000 active phones, the modeled all-in envelope is DKK 49k–55k/year with the assumed labour allowance, versus DKK 1.2k–6.6k cash infrastructure. If subsidy or unrestricted committed funding falls short, the chosen paid capacity provides a more repeatable source of income. If families predominantly use one phone, do not assume the plan will fund the work. Reassess the operating budget and use setup or funded cohorts to close the gap.

| Months from start | Deliverable and decision gate |
|---|---|
