Plainphone for parents is where you set the rules for a child's phone that runs Plainphone. You choose the apps, who can call and text, and when school time and bedtime start. Your child sees the same rules on their phone.

This is an alpha. It works, but it is new and will change. Try it with a phone you can test on before you rely on it.

What you can do
• Choose which apps open, and set daily time limits.
• Approve who can call and text. 112, emergency numbers and you can always be reached.
• Set school time and bedtime.
• Answer requests for more time, a new app or a new contact.
• See where the phone is when you ask. Your child sees that you asked.
• Make the phone ring, ask "Are you okay?" and get SOS alerts.
• Switch each feature on or off for each phone.
• Share a phone with the other parent in your household.

The child's phone needs the Plainphone phone app. You pair them by scanning the code the phone shows. How to set up the child's phone is at plainphone.org.

What it is not
Plainphone adds friction. It does not make a phone safe, and the web filter does not catch everything. Each setting says what it does and what it cannot do.

Your data
You sign in with a link sent to your email. We keep your email address, your household and the rules for each phone. Most history is deleted after 30 days. You can delete your account in Settings. No ads, no analytics, and nothing is sent to us for any other purpose.

Open source and free
Plainphone is open source (Apache-2.0) and free. You can run your own server. More at plainphone.org.
