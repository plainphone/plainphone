# Plainphone trademark policy

The Plainphone code is open source under the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE). You may use, change, sell and distribute it. The license does not give you rights to the name "Plainphone" or the Plainphone logo (Apache-2.0, section 6). This policy explains what you can do with them.

The goal is simple: people should always be able to tell the official Plainphone apart from products built on it.

## You may, without asking

- Say that your product is **"based on Plainphone"** or **"built with Plainphone"**, as long as your product has its own name.
- Use the name "Plainphone" to refer to the project in articles, talks, reviews, tutorials and comparisons.
- Run an unmodified, self-hosted Plainphone server and call it Plainphone, as long as it is clear that you run it, not the Plainphone project.
- Link to plainphone.org.

## You may not, without written permission

- Call your product, app, service, company or domain "Plainphone", or a name that can be confused with it (e.g. "Plainphone Pro", "PlainPhone Kids", "plainphone-app.com").
- Publish a modified version in an app store under the name or logo "Plainphone".
- Use the logo in a way that suggests the Plainphone project made, endorses or supports your product.

## Credit

Derived products must keep the [NOTICE](NOTICE) file and show its content where third-party notices usually appear, for example on an "About" or "Licenses" screen.

## Questions

Open an issue at https://github.com/plainphone/plainphone or use the contact address on plainphone.org. We are friendly and usually say yes to good-faith uses.
